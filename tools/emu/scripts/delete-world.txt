# The delete-a-world flow, end to end: the picker's new legend row, the card
# with the world it is about to remove standing beside it, the hold part way
# through, the confirmation, and the offer to put it back.
#
# Emulation has no flashcart, so this script only runs against a build with
# the storage preview hack applied -- see docs/emulator-screenshots.md on why
# nothing that touches a save can be tested here.

wait 900
shot world-select       # picker with [A] PLAY / [Z] DELETE under the list

press Z 8
wait 30
shot delete-card        # the card, armed, world orbiting beside it

stick 0 0 10 A
shot delete-holding     # ground part way gone

stick 0 0 100 A
wait 20
shot deleted            # back on the picker: WORLD DELETED, [Z] RESTORE

wait 180
shot restore-offer      # the toast has gone; the offer has not

press Z 8
wait 30
shot restored

stop
