# The home marker on the compass, from the one place it is guaranteed to be
# hidden -- spawn is home -- out to where it has a bearing to point at.
#
# The player spawns standing on home facing north, so the first shot is the
# empty rim: within a couple of blocks the marker is suppressed rather than
# left to spin on the player's own footsteps.  Walking north is what brings
# the wedge out, and it comes out pinned, because home is now directly behind.
# The turn after that is what the marker is for: the same fixed point closing
# on the index until the two touch, which is what facing home looks like.
#
# The turn is deliberately two different speeds.  Full deflection steers about
# 48 degrees per six rendered frames, which is a third of the strip in one
# step and cannot stop anywhere near the middle of it; 45 counts is a little
# over 20 degrees in the same six, and the shaping in player.c is why the
# smaller number is so much gentler than its size suggests.

wait 400
press START 8

wait 500
press START 8           # CREATE WORLD keyboard

wait 60
press START 8

wait 240
shot at-home            # standing on home: rim empty, nothing to point at

stick 0 70 240          # walk north, away from home
wait 30
shot walked-away        # home behind: wedge pinned to the end of the strip

stick 70 0 20           # come about, bringing home back onto the strip
wait 12
shot on-strip           # off to the right, sliding in from the pin

stick 45 0 6
wait 12
shot facing-home        # wedge under the index: home is straight ahead

stick 45 0 6
wait 12
shot turned-past        # carried on turning: home slides out to the left

stop
