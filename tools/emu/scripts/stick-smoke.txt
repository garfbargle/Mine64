# Smoke test for the reshaped stick: does the player still walk, still steer,
# still aim with Z, and does a barely-deflected stick now do almost nothing.
#
# This checks that the control path works, not how it feels -- frame pacing
# under the emulator is nothing like the console's, and the whole point of the
# rate limiter and the split look delta is what they do at a real frame rate.

wait 960                # title preview: menuAct will not launch before it
press A 8
wait 40                 # create-world card
press START 8
wait 60                 # name keyboard
press START 8
wait 300                # into the world

wait 24
shot 1-spawn            # heading before anything moves

# Full deflection forward.  Top walk speed is unchanged, so this should cover
# the same ground the old build did.
stick 0 80 60
wait 12
shot 2-walked

# Full deflection sideways is a steer: about 204 degrees a second, so 60
# emulator frames at ~23 fps should come most of the way round.
stick 80 0 60
wait 12
shot 3-steered

# Z-look down, then back up.  Pitch is deliberately slower than yaw now.
stick 0 -80 20 Z
wait 8
shot 4-aimed-down
stick 0 80 20 Z
wait 8
shot 5-aimed-back

# The point of the exercise: a stick resting just outside the old dead zone.
# 12 counts used to turn about 33 degrees a second; it should now be a crawl,
# and 8 counts should be nothing at all.
stick 12 0 60
wait 12
shot 6-barely-steered
stick 8 0 60
wait 12
shot 7-dead-zone

stop
