# A thirty-second demo reel, meant to be screen-recorded off the emulator.
#
#   RES=640x480 tools/emu/run.sh tools/emu/scripts/demo-30s.txt
#
# The first ~50 seconds are preroll: the title card builds its preview world,
# the create-world card is accepted, and the world is entered.  Start recording
# when the world appears -- the demo proper opens on a one-second still, then
# runs about thirty seconds and stops.
#
# Durations are rendered frames, and the emulator draws roughly 23 of them a
# second in the world, so a second is ~23 frames.  The times in the beat
# headings are measured from the first frame of the world.
#
# NOTHING HERE MAY DEPEND ON THE TERRAIN.  The seed is `(u32) osGetTime()` read
# once at the title (menu.c, menuPendingSeed), which is not reproducible under
# mupen64plus: the same script gives a different world from one run to the
# next.  An earlier draft of this file walked to a particular tree and felled
# it, which was worth watching exactly as long as the seed held still.  So
# every beat below works anywhere: the ground is always within reach when you
# look down, the pack always opens, and the camera always turns.
#
# What that costs is the tree: felling one needs a trunk within the six-block
# reach, and no fixed heading guarantees a trunk.  Mining is shown on the
# ground instead.  If a demo has to show whole-tree felling and a real craft,
# it has to be re-tuned against the world that boots that day.

# --- preroll: build and enter the world (not part of the thirty seconds) ---
wait 960                # the title preview: menuAct will not launch before it
press A 8
wait 40                 # create-world card: terrain, mods, seed
press START 8
wait 60                 # name keyboard
press START 8
wait 300                # dropped into the world that was orbiting the menu

# --- 1. where you woke up (0:00) ---
wait 24
shot 1-spawn
stick 25 0 100          # come about 150 degrees: horizon, fog, compass, HUD
wait 20
shot 2-horizon

# --- 2. out into it (0:04) ---
# Walks are kept short and the heading is changed between them.  A blind
# script cannot see a hillside coming, and forty blocks committed to one
# heading is how the camera ends up pressed into a wall of dirt for the rest
# of the take.
stick 0 70 26           # the walk is the point: columns stream in ahead
stick -16 70 22         # drift the heading so it does not read as a rail
wait 10
shot 3-walked

# --- 3. mine (0:09) ---
stick 0 -48 10 Z        # Z-look down: the stick turns the head, not the body
wait 8
shot 4-aimed
press B 22              # ground is 22 ticks, ~9 frames of held B.  Two blocks,
                        # not more: the ray drops to the block beneath each one
                        # that goes, so a long hold digs a pit and films it
                        # from the bottom.  One block alone is worse -- placed
                        # straight back it leaves an empty pack.
wait 34                 # they pop out, settle, and pull in
shot 5-mined

# --- 4. put one back (0:13) ---
press A 8               # place the block that just went in the pack
wait 12
stick 0 -45 14          # step back off it, so the placed block is seen from
                        # outside rather than through the camera's nose
stick 0 43 8 Z          # and lift the view back toward the horizon
wait 8
shot 6-placed

# --- 5. the pack (0:15) ---
press START 8
wait 26
shot 7-pack
stick 60 0 40           # hold right: the cursor walks out of the hotbar and
                        # into POCKET CRAFT, where right does nothing more
wait 12
shot 8-craft            # PLANKS / STICK / CRAFTING TABLE / TORCH and their cost
stick 0 -60 8           # one row down
wait 16
press B 8
wait 16

# --- 6. third person, and run (0:21) ---
stick 0 43 5 Z          # level the camera again: -29 down, +18 back, +11 here
press CUP 8             # third person: the avatar, and a nine-block reach
wait 16
shot 9-third
stick 0 70 55 L         # L sprints, and sprinting spends food
stick 0 70 10 L+R       # L+R into a stride is the vault; clear of one, a jump
wait 8
stick 19 70 45 L        # lean into the turn rather than snapping round
stick 0 70 40 L
shot 10-run
stick -22 0 38          # settle on the view
wait 16
shot 11-final

stop
